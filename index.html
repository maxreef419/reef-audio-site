REEF is an independent, full-service music and sound studio for brands, agencies and productions. We create original music, sound design and sonic identities — bringing creative direction, production and final delivery together under one roof.

Selected work

Capabilities

Helping brands and agencies define the right sonic direction before production begins. From creative consulting and reference analysis to music strategy and production planning.

From the first musical idea to the final master, we compose and produce original music tailored to each project’s creative and production needs. Whether it’s a simple electronic track or a full-scale production with live musicians, orchestras, and choirs, we build the right solution for the brief.

Purpose-built sound design that adds texture, clarity, and emotion. Every sound is crafted to support the visuals rather than compete with them.

Distinctive sonic identities that help brands become instantly recognizable across advertising, digital products, retail environments, and customer experiences.

An embedded audio team working alongside brands, agencies, and production companies. Strategic support, faster decision-making, consistent quality, and seamless collaboration across multiple projects.

About

Built on both sides of the brief.

01

Agency-Side Perspective

Founded by composer and producer Max Kouznichenkov , REEF brings two decades across music and advertising — including ten years inside Leo Burnett, Saatchi & Saatchi and Grey. We read briefs the way they were written, and we know what it takes to get the idea through production and approvals intact.

02

In From the Start

We come in at script and animatic stage, not at the end. That means the musical direction is locked early, references stop competing with each other, and the edit isn't rebuilt around a track that arrived too late. Fewer rounds, fewer surprises, same idea from first presentation to final master.

03

One Track to a Sonic System

Two decades in the industry have given us a trusted network of composers, producers, musicians, vocalists, sound designers and engineers — so we build the right team for each project, from a single track to a complete sonic identity across markets and formats.
