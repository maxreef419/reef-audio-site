About

Built on both sides of the brief.

Led by award-winning composer and producer Max Kouznichenkov , REEF understands both sides of the brief. Ten years inside Leo Burnett, Saatchi & Saatchi and Grey taught us how ideas move through agencies, production and approvals — and how to keep them intact.

After twenty years in music and advertising, we have built a trusted circle of producers, musicians, vocalists, award-winning sound designers and engineers with leading international credits. REEF scales strategically around each brief, bringing in the right people at the right stage while keeping one clear creative direction from first idea to final master.
